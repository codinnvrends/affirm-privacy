Privacy Policy for Affirm!

Effective Date: 29 October 2026
Last Updated: 29 October 2026

Introduction

Sapps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Affirm! mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

In short: Affirm! has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and we run no servers that receive your data. What you enter stays on your device, except where you or your phone's own services move it, as described below.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, receive or have access to:

  • Your name, email address or any account details (the App has no accounts)
  • Your profile, preferences, moods, favorites or affirmations
  • Your voice recordings
  • Usage analytics, advertising identifiers or location

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device so that it can work:

  • Your personalization choices (age group, gender, focus areas) and daily mood
  • Favorites, viewing history, streaks and your daily card draws
  • Affirmations you write yourself
  • Reminder and notification settings
  • App settings such as theme, language, sound and widget look
  • Voice recordings you choose to make of yourself reading affirmations

This information is used only to personalize affirmations, remember your settings, show your progress, play your recordings and send the reminders you set up.

When Information Leaves Your Device

Android backup to your Google account

On Android, the App takes part in Android's standard backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may save the App's database and settings to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling. Voice recordings are not included in this cloud backup, but they are included when you move to a new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account and Google's privacy policy; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Backup files you export

In Settings → Backup you can save a backup file containing your data and voice recordings. The App creates this file only when you ask, and you choose where it goes (for example, a cloud drive, email or another app) through your phone's share sheet. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. Restoring a backup file only reads the file you pick.

Reading affirmations aloud

When you tap Listen on an affirmation you haven't recorded, the App passes its text to your phone's text-to-speech engine (for example, Google's Speech Services). Depending on the engine and your phone's settings, it may process the text online or download voices. That processing is done by the engine's provider under its own privacy policy.

On-device AI ("Write it for me")

On supported Android phones, suggestions are written by Gemini Nano, which runs on your device through Google's ML Kit. The topic you choose is not sent to us. Your phone may download the model from Google, and Google's ML Kit and system services may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information as described in Google's terms. On other phones, suggestions come from templates built into the App.

Sharing

When you share an affirmation, the App creates an image and hands it, with the text, to the app you choose. What happens next is up to that app.

Permissions

Affirm! may ask for or use:

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set up
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to deliver reminders on time on Android 12+
  • Microphone: only when you record yourself reading an affirmation
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminder schedule after the phone restarts
  • Vibration: for reminder alerts
  • Internet: the App itself does not send your data to us; network access is used by the components described above, such as downloading voices or the AI model

You can change or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. The App works without the microphone and notification permissions; only those features are unavailable.

Children's Privacy

Affirm! is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents should be aware that recordings and backups are stored on the device and in any backup locations described above.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: uninstalling the App deletes everything it stored. You can also remove individual favorites, recordings and affirmations in the App.
  • Android backup: you can delete the App's backup from your Google account's backup settings, or turn backup off.
  • Exported backup files: delete them from wherever you saved them.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. We will post the new version and update the "Last Updated" date above.
